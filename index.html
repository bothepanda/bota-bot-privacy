bota-bot

bota-bot is a personal tool of Botagoz Kaukenova. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it does. It reads Botagoz's own Gmail and Google Calendar to prepare her a private morning summary on her own home server. It only reads: it does not send e-mail and does not change or delete anything.

Privacy. The data is used only to make that summary for Botagoz. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
